# Theo Xavier

**`Software Engineering Student | Java Back-End Development`**

I’m Theo De Melo Xavier, a 22-year-old from Rio de Janeiro. My first contact with technology came during my technical program in Information Technology at Senac Amazonas, an experience that sparked my interest in the field and shaped my career choice. I’m currently studying Software Engineering at CEUB, where I’m building a solid foundation in software development and deepening my knowledge of the field.

I’m especially interested in Java back-end development and curious about cybersecurity, with a focus on understanding application architecture, the logic behind systems, and the challenges of information security. My goal is to develop technical skills that allow me to build efficient, secure, and well-structured solutions.

Outside of technology, I find in literature another way to explore ideas and broaden my perspectives. I’m driven by curiosity, by the desire to uncover what I don’t yet know, and by a constant pursuit of learning.
